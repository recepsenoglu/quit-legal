Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. In short

Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no server of ours. We never sell your data and never use your habits to profile you.

2. What is stored on your device

The habits you track, when you quit, how much you used and what it cost, your "why" note and photo, restarts and their notes, SOS sessions, savings goals and your settings. Everything is stored encrypted (AES-256); the key is kept in your phone's secure keystore. An app lock PIN is never stored, only a salted hash.

3. What we don't collect

No location, contacts, analytics or tracking of how you use the app. Your habit and health data is never sent to any advertising or analytics service.

4. Ads in the free version

Free users see one banner on the milestones screen, served by Google AdMob. The ad request contains no habit data. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and basic device information. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change it any time under Settings → Ad privacy options. Pro users see no ads and the ad SDK is not started. Google's practices: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. To check whether Pro is active we use RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records. We never see your card details.

6. Notifications and the widget

Reminders are scheduled on your device and only after you turn them on. In discreet mode (on by default) notifications and the home screen widget never show the habit's name.

7. Backups and sharing

An exported backup is a plain, unencrypted file that goes wherever you choose to save or send it. The progress card is only shared when you share it. The app turns off the phone's automatic cloud backup for its data.

8. Your rights

You can see and take all your data with Settings → Export data, and erase it with Settings → Delete everything (or by uninstalling the app). These are always free. This covers your rights under GDPR (Art. 15–20) and KVKK (Art. 11) for the data on your device.

9. How long data is kept

On your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by the store and RevenueCat as long as required for billing and the law.

The app deals with topics such as alcohol and gambling and is not meant for people under 18.
